Marketing and PR

I have marketed itineraries through company websites, written content for the companies I worked with, and promoted cruises to group travel clients while building long client relationships. My MBA (2022 to 2024) gives that work a framework.

  • Itinerary and destination content
  • Cruise and group travel promotion
  • Client relationships and communication
  • Brand voice for travel businesses

The two sides use the same skill. I find out what a traveller really wants, then show it to them clearly enough that they say yes.
